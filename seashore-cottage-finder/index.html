🏖️ Seashore Cottage Finder

Find open dates for a Premium Cottage (2 Bedroom) at Sun Retreats Seashore — scan the whole summer instead of one date at a time.

How this works. The resort's own booking page only lets you check one date range at a time, which makes hunting for vacancy painful. This tool builds a checklist of every stay window across the summer, gives you a one-click link straight into the booking page for each, and remembers which dates you found open — all saved in your browser.

Why can't it just check availability automatically?

The resort runs its booking through Campspot. Real-time availability lives behind a paid, authenticated API and bot protection, so a free page like this usually cannot read it directly from your browser (the request gets blocked by the site's security/CORS rules). The tool will still attempt a live check when you ask — but the dependable path is: open each window's link, glance at the result, and tick it off here. You can also teach it your booking link below so the one-click links land on the exact dates automatically.

Advanced — live-check settings (for the technically curious)

The live auto-check tries to fetch each booking link from your browser. Cross-site security usually blocks this. If you have a CORS proxy you trust, you can route requests through it. Leave blank to attempt a direct request.

Your settings, learned link, and date statuses are stored only in this browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere except the booking links you open and any live-check requests you trigger.
